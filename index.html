Our Story

From Aadiwasi FC
to TFC Temni.

The club's story is rooted in tribal and rural talent, community effort and consistent player development. Public reporting in September 2026 described Temni as a growing football nursery producing district, divisional, state and national-level players.

Identity

A grassroots club with a bigger mission

The club began under the name Aadiwasi Football Club Temni and now competes and develops talent as Temni Football Club. The current crest keeps a strong local football identity while the digital platform is built to support scouting, player profiles, trials, news, sponsors and community participation.

Player Development

A pathway, not just practice.

The structure takes cues from leading Indian football academies: RFYC's holistic multi-disciplinary model, BBFS's age-based coaching pathway and Zinc Football's scouting plus player-performance approach.

01

Grassroots

Fun, movement, ball mastery and love for the game for younger players.

02

Development

Technical fundamentals, game understanding, fitness and regular match exposure.

03

Competitive

Position-specific coaching, tournament preparation, video review and measurable targets.

04

Elite Pathway

District, divisional, state, national camps, scouting visibility and career guidance.

Website and academy information architecture benchmarked against leading Indian football development models: Reliance Foundation Young Champs, Bhaichung Bhutia Football Schools and Zinc Football Academy. TFC keeps the same clarity of pathway while preserving its own local identity.
Verified 2026 Coverage

Temni talent is moving up.

These highlights come from a September 27, 2026 news report on Temni Football Club. Player positions are not guessed where the report does not specify them.

JUNIOR NATIONAL

Three TFC girls in the Madhya Pradesh pathway

Tamanna Watti • Pinky Uikey • Saraswati Bhalavi

Reported to have earned places in the Madhya Pradesh team pathway after district representation and a national camp in Indore, with the Junior National competition in Narayanpur, Chhattisgarh.

U-14 STATE LEVEL

Temni players representing Narmadapuram division

Sunakshi Bhalavi, Vaishali Dhurve, Kavya Watti, Yathika Watti, Amrita Sariam, Sohani Dhurve and Tapasya Dhurve were reported in the girls' group; Sunakshi was named captain. Onish Dhurve and Mayank Chawde were reported in the boys' group.

BEYOND THE PITCH

Football opening career pathways

The report also noted that a TFC woman footballer had been selected into Madhya Pradesh Police after having previously participated in a Senior National camp — showing how sport can support broader life opportunities.
